Privacy Policy

Nagesh Social Media - Lifetime Version · Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains how Nagesh Social Media - Lifetime Version (“the app”) handles information when you use it.

Information the app handles

  • Account and profile information: Supabase authentication account identifiers and session data, plus profile details such as your name, username, bio, avatar, and referral information.
  • Content you submit: Posts, stories, likes, profile photos, and images you choose to upload. Uploaded media may be publicly accessible through links used by the app.
  • App activity and account data: Social content interactions, referral counts, wallet balances, and payout requests, including payout details you submit.
  • Local storage: Authentication session data and certain app preferences or request data may be stored on your device.
  • Advertising data: The app uses Google AdMob. Google and its partners may process device identifiers and advertising or diagnostic information under their own policies and your device settings.

How information is used and shared

Information is used to provide sign-in, profiles, social features, media uploads, referrals, wallet and payout-request features, app security, and advertising. Supabase provides authentication, database, and storage services. Google AdMob provides advertising services. These providers process information to provide their services under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Profile details and content submitted for social features may be visible to other users. Do not post information you do not want to share. Payout details are used to process or review payout requests.

Retention and your choices

Information is retained as needed to operate the app, maintain account records, address disputes, and meet applicable legal requirements. You can limit advertising personalization in your device settings. To request access to or deletion of account information, contact the developer using the contact details on the app’s Google Play listing. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate operational purposes.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the developer through the app’s Google Play listing.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app’s data practices change. The current version will be published at this URL. For privacy questions or requests, use the developer contact details shown on the app’s Google Play listing.
